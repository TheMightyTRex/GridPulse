Widgets for your website

Show live electricity figures on your own site, free. Each widget is a small box that updates itself every 5 minutes and links back to Grid Pulse.

Make a widget

Using the widgets

  • They are free, for any site, with no sign-up.
  • Please keep the Grid Pulse link in the widget. It also names the source of the figures.
  • The widget is 300 by 150 pixels, but you can change the width and height in the code.
  • It loads nothing from other sites and sets no cookies.

Want the figures themselves? See Use our data.
