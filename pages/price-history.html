Historic prices

The GB wholesale electricity price, from the past day back to the full run of available history. Every chart below is labelled with the exact calendar period it covers - not just a range name - so you always know what you are looking at. New to the price figure itself? Read what it means and how it is set first.

Price history

GB wholesale market index price (£/MWh, Elexon's MID from APX - a short-term traded price, not the day-ahead auction), sourced from the Elexon Insights Solution at up to 30-minute resolution, aggregated to the granularity shown at longer ranges. Longer ranges will fill in as more history is collected.

About this data

Kate Morley's original National Grid: Live has tracked GB system data since the site launched, and its "All time" view is the closest precedent for the range above - here, "All time" runs back as far as Elexon's BMRS history goes, with older data aggregated into coarser (e.g. monthly or seasonal) points to keep charts readable.

Wholesale prices are volatile day to day and were exceptionally high during the 2021–2023 global gas price crisis, so long-range charts are usually shown on their own scale rather than forced to match the short-range ones.
